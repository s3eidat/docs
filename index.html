      A word processor that keeps your documents on your device

      Real pages

      Write on A4, Letter, Legal or A5 sheets with your margins, page breaks, headers and footers with page numbers, and a table of contents that knows which page each heading is on. What you see is what prints.

      Everything you format

      Styles (Title, Headings, Quote, Code), fonts on your device, sizes, colours, highlights, alignment and justify, spacing, indents, bullets, numbers, checklists, tables with merged and shaded cells, images, links, and Arabic right-to-left paragraphs.

      Open and save .docx

      Open .docx files and save them back, or export PDF (print), Markdown, HTML and plain text. Documents autosave in this browser, it works offline, and nothing is ever uploaded.

      No account, no server, no analytics. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes your documents, so download a .docx of anything important.
